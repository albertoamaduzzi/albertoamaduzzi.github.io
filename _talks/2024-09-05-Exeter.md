We investigate the emergence of large-scale traffic jams as a first-order phase transition in the number of vehicles trapped within the road network. We explore the connection between this critical behavior and the origin-destination (OD) structure of mobility flows. To this end, we employ a GPU-accelerated microscopic multi-lane traffic simulator, analyzing transitions across various synthetic regional OD scenarios
