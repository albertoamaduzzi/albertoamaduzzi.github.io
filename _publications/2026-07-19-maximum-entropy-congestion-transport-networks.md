Presented at the World Conference on Information Systems and Technologies (WorldCIST 2026), Funchal, Portugal. Published in *Recent Trends and Challenges in Information Systems and Technologies*, Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems, vol. 2091, pp. 90&ndash;97, Springer, Cham. DOI: [10.1007/978-3-032-32029-2_8](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-32029-2_8).

Authors: Alberto Amaduzzi, Lorenzo Di Meco, Gregorio Berselli, Davide Micheli, Aldo Vannelli, Armando Bazzani.

The paper illustrates a road map to build a data-driven Markov process that simulates congestion formation on a transport network using a priori information, with preliminary results on the real case of Bologna.
